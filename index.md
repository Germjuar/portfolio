# German Juarez

## Bio
Hi, I’m German Juarez, a Data Science student at Oregon State University driven by my passion for storytelling and social equity. 

Growing up as a Hispanic first-generation student in a rural community, I navigated systemic setbacks all the time. Those experiences shaped my core mission: to use data as a tool to improve society and advocate for others.

I have always loved to write, and I view data science as another medium for storytelling. To me, there is immense power in statistics. I consider it a privilege to be a steward of information, taking complex, intimidating datasets and translating them into something anyone can understand.

Today, I apply this mindset to support local non-profits and public policy organizations I volunteer with. My focus is on making information actionable. By empowering community leaders to make impactful decisions, my goal is to turn these complicated numbers into compelling stories that help create a world with tangibly better outcomes for everyone.

## Skills
•	**Statistical Analysis & Modeling:** Utilizing R and Python for multiple linear regression and multivariate analysis to find insights from complex public and economic datasets. Finding the right method for the question and communicating it in plain language. 

•	**Data Storytelling & Dashboards:** Transforming quantitative findings into accessible, self-service decision aids. Proficient in crafting interactive GIS demographic dashboards and narrative-driven articles to educate general audiences and empower non-profits. 

•	**Predictive Modeling:** Engineering data pipelines and forecasting trends using Pandas and Scikit-Learn. Deploying algorithms like K-Nearest Neighbors regression to build reliable models. 

•	**Survey Methodology & Data Collection:** Designing accessible surveys tailored for vulnerable demographics. Managing rigorous data cleaning and evaluating sampling to ensure accurate, representative models. 

•	**Inclusive Leadership & Mentorship:** Fostering collaborative, equitable environments in technical spaces. Using my background to mentor peers, lead cross-functional workshops, and drive community engagement initiatives. 

## Projects
Here is a selection of my recent data science projects, applying statistical modeling and data storytelling to real-world datasets:

•	**K-Shaped Economy Scrollytelling Article:** Built comparative multiple linear regression models in R using BLS and Federal Reserve data to analyze the U.S. wealth gap, translating complex economic disparities into an accessible, interactive web narrative.

•	**NBA Player Projection Model:** Engineered a Python and Pandas data pipeline, implementing a K-Nearest Neighbors algorithm to forecast statistics for the active league and evaluate projected simulated player values for the following season.

•	**Election Survey Statistical Weighting:** Analyzed ANES election data in R with a University of Michigan undergraduate cohort to demonstrate the critical impact of sampling design and statistical weights on generating representative population models.
